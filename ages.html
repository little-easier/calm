Who it's for

Calm Spaces is a set of gentle sensory play spaces: bubbles, glowing ink, a moonlit glade and a koi pond.

In short

  • Rated 4+, in the App Store's Kids section for ages 5 and under.
  • Nothing to read and nothing to win or lose. One touch is enough to play.
  • Made with children with SEND in mind, whatever their age.
  • No ads, no purchases, no accounts, no chat and no links out of the app.

Ages 5 and under

Every space answers a single touch: hold to blow a bubble, tap to pop it, swirl a finger through glowing ink, tap the glade for sparkles or feed the koi. There are no words to read, no instructions to follow and no way to get it wrong, so very young children can play on their own from the start.

Older children and grown-ups

Older children enjoy the slower discoveries: filling a shape with ink, skimming stones across the pond, finding a rare koi or holding still until the butterflies land. Plenty of grown-ups use it for a quiet minute too.

Children with SEND, of any age

Calm Spaces was designed with SEND families in mind. There are no high scores, nothing to beat and no failing. A little count cheers on a run of pops or skips, then it's gone. Sounds are soft, surprises can be made rarer or switched off, and the buttons can be hidden if they become a distraction.

Sessions end gently. A grown-up can set a wind-down timer: sound and light fade over half a minute, then each space says goodnight. Nothing stops suddenly.

Grown-up settings

In each play space, tap the ••• button in the top corner. You'll be asked to tap a number written in words, so only a grown-up can get in. Inside are the wind-down timer, volume, how often surprises happen and which buttons show.

Help and questions

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything is built into the app and works fully offline. Nothing leaves the device (see the privacy policy).

Can I turn the sounds or surprises down?

Yes. The buttons at the top change the scene and sounds freely, and the grown-up settings set the volume and how often surprises happen, including off.

How do I end a session without upset?

Set the wind-down timer in the grown-up settings before handing it over. When the time is up, everything fades slowly and says goodnight, and only a grown-up can start again.
